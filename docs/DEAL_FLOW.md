# Add: the deal + commission screen (small addition)

The app currently ends at "connect." Add one screen after connect that shows how a deal completes and when the platform earns. This makes the business model visible in the demo. Do NOT change anything else.

## New screen: "Deal room" / deal summary (after Connect)

After the sponsor clicks Connect and sees the confirmation, add a simple next screen (or expand the confirmation) that shows the deal lifecycle and where the commission sits.

Show a clear 4-step status tracker (visual, horizontal or vertical):

1. **Connected** ✓ (done) — "Intro sent to [club]. Free."
2. **Negotiating** (active) — "Agree what's included and the price." Show the club's typical deal range as the starting point.
3. **Deal agreed** — a field/summary showing an agreed deal value (for the demo, prefill an example, e.g. €8,000). 
4. **Live** — sponsorship active, reporting begins.

## The commission panel (this is the money moment)

On the same screen, show a clean breakdown box that makes the model obvious:

- Agreed deal value: €8,000
- Platform commission (this tier): e.g. 10% on deals under €10k → €800  (use 2% if the example deal is large)
- "You pay us nothing until this point. We only earn when the deal closes."
- If the club qualifies for tax relief, show the tax line too so the sponsor sees net cost.

Make it explicit and honest: free to discover, free to connect, free to negotiate — commission only on the agreed deal value at close.

## Optional: lock-in value (answers disintermediation)

Under the commission box, a short list of what the platform does at close, so it's clear why they stay:
- "We generate the sponsorship agreement."
- "We produce the tax-compliance documentation." (for qualifying clubs)
- "We track reach and ROI through the season."
- "We surface your next matches."

## Rules

- Do NOT change the quiz, matching, data, or existing screens.
- This is ONE new screen after Connect. Keep the same visual style.
- Use a clear example deal value so the commission math is concrete in the demo.
- Copy must say: commission only on closed deals, never on contact.
