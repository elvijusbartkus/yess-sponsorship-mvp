# Simplify for a 1-minute demo + add the curation layer

Two jobs. (1) Radically simplify the app so the whole story is showable in ~60 seconds. (2) Add the "curation / marketing strategy" layer (an Olympic Committee mentor's idea) as the differentiator and anti-leakage lock-in.

## PART 1 — SIMPLIFY (priority — this is what makes the demo work)

The app has too much text and too many steps for a 1-minute demo. Cut ruthlessly.

### Principles
- Every screen: one clear thing, one clear CTA. Big, obvious, minimal words.
- Remove explanatory paragraphs. A demo is watched, not read. Show, don't tell.
- Fewer steps. The demo path should be: land → pick sample sponsor OR quick quiz → matches → one match → deal. Under 6 clicks total.
- Big buttons, big headlines, little body text.

### Specific cuts
- Landing: one headline, two big buttons (sponsor / club). Cut the long subtext to one short line. Cut the footer paragraph to one line.
- Quiz: keep it fast. Consider reducing to 3-4 questions for the demo, or allow a "sample sponsor" one-click that skips the quiz entirely and jumps to matches. The quiz should be optional for speed.
- Match cards: name, sport/region, one big number (reach), FIT score, ONE short reason line. Cut everything else to the detail page.
- Detail page: trim hard. Audience numbers, "what you get" (short), deal range, ONE line on value. Remove long explanatory blocks.
- THE DEAL / END SCREEN — simplify the MOST. Right now it has too many boxes and text. Reduce to: agreed value (big), commission (one line), "club receives X" (one line), done. Three numbers, no paragraphs. This is the money moment — make it instant and clear, not a wall of text.

### The 1-minute demo path (design the app so this flows in ~60s)
1. Landing — "back sport" button. (3s)
2. One-click sample sponsor (skip quiz) OR 3 fast taps. (10s)
3. Loading "matching…" (2s)
4. Matches appear — point at the top match. (10s)
5. Click match → clean detail. (10s)
6. Connect → membership gate (one line: €X/mo to connect). (10s)
7. Deal room → three numbers: deal value, our commission, club gets. Done. (10s)

Every screen must be scannable in 2-3 seconds. If a judge has to READ, it's too much.

## PART 2 — ADD the curation / strategy layer (the differentiator)

An Olympic Committee mentor suggested: we don't just introduce sponsor and club, we curate the ongoing marketing strategy between sponsor, club, and athlete — because athletes are focused on training, not on selling themselves. This is our anti-leakage lock-in and our key value-add.

### Add to the product
- On the deal/close screen or match detail, a section: "We curate the campaign" — showing that the platform builds and runs the marketing (posts, stories, content) for the sponsorship, so the club/athlete doesn't have to.
- Use the LLM to GENERATE example marketing content live: given a sponsor + club match, generate a sample social post and a story caption for the sponsorship. This is demoable and impressive ("watch it draft the campaign in 2 seconds").
- Frame it as: "We're not just an introduction. We curate the sponsor–club–athlete relationship and run the marketing, so the value keeps flowing and the deal stays on our platform."

### Why this matters (for pitch, not UI)
- Anti-leakage: ongoing curation service = they can't just dip after the intro.
- Value-add: solves that athletes/clubs can't market themselves.
- Differentiator: Sponsoo is a directory; we're a curated service layer.

Keep the UI for this SIMPLE too — one section, a "generate campaign" button, show the generated post/story. Don't add heavy UI.

## Rules
- Simplification is the priority. A clean 60-second flow beats a feature-rich slow one.
- The curation layer should be ONE simple, demoable moment (generate a post/story), not a complex module.
- Less text everywhere. Big CTAs. Scannable screens.
- The end/deal screen must be the simplest screen, three numbers, no paragraphs.
